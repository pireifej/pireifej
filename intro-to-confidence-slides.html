The Great Confidence Mystery

The Grocery Store Test

Start by asking the group:

  • "Raise your hand if you bought a bag of 'Confidence' at the grocery store this morning?"
  • "Which aisle was it in? Frozen foods? Cereal?"
  • "Is confidence something you can buy? Is it something you are born with? Or is it something you BUILD?"

It doesn't mean you never make a mistake

It doesn't mean you never feel nervous

Speaker's Toolkit

Body Stretch

Getting Loose

  1. Reach for the Sky: "Inhale and reach up as high as you can to the ceiling... now drop down."
  2. Shoulder Rolls: "Roll your shoulders back. We carry stress in our shoulders - let it go."
  3. The Shake Out: "Shake out your right hand, left hand. Right foot, left foot. Get the wiggles out!"
Speaker's Toolkit

Face & Voice Yoga

  1. Silly Faces: "Make your face as BIG as a lion! Now scrunch it as small as a lemon!"
  2. Jaw Massage: "Gently massage your jaw. A tight jaw makes it hard to speak clearly."
  3. Lip Rolls: "Blow out through your lips making a horse sound: Brrrbrrrbrrr. This wakes up your voice."
Speaker's Toolkit

Breathing Control

The Engine

Belly Breathing

"Put one hand on your belly. When we breathe, we want the belly to move OUT, not the shoulders up."

Box Breathing

"Inhale for 4 counts... Hold for 4 counts... Exhale for 4 counts. Feel your belly move."

Taming the "Fear Monster"

Meet the Fear Monster

The Truth: "Feeling butterflies in your stomach is NORMAL! Even famous people and celebrities get scared before they go on stage."

Reframe It

Butterflies = Energy

"Fear is just your body giving you extra energy to do something great. The butterflies aren't bad - we just need to make them fly in formation."
Fear Monster

Techniques to Tame
the Butterflies

Reframe It

"When you feel that shaky feeling, tell yourself: 'I'm not nervous, I'm EXCITED!'"

Find a Friend

"Look for friendly faces in the audience who are smiling at you. Ignore the grumpy chairs."

Activity

The Power Pose

Instructions: "We are going to stand up and take up space! Put your hands on your hips, chest out, and chin up like a Superhero."
  1. Stand tall with feet shoulder-width apart
  2. Hands on hips, chest out, chin up
  3. Hold this pose for 2 minutes
  4. Feel the confidence growing!
Pro Tip

Turn Up the Courage

Play upbeat music while they hold the pose. This tricks your brain into feeling brave!

What's Your Message?

Audience Analysis

"Always ask: Who am I talking to? You talk differently to your best friend than you do to the school principal."
PuppyVS. Principal
The Scenario: Say "Hello" and ask them to sit down

Round 1: Talk to a Puppy

High-pitched, exaggerated, simple words.

"Hiiii buddy! Who's a good boy?! Sit! Siiiit!"

The Scenario: Say "Hello" and ask them to sit down

Round 2: Talk to the Principal

Respectful, lower pitch, formal.

"Good morning. Please, take a seat."

The LessonWe naturally change our voice to fit who is listening. Speaking to an audience is just choosing the right voice!
