03 / Action bullet instruction card

The Bullet Point Formula

ACTION VERB

+

CORE TASK /
WHAT YOU DID

+

TOOL USED
or RESULT

Action verb: Strong start — Operated, Prepared, Resolved, Guided.

Core task: What you were assigned to handle.

Result or tool: Equipment used OR why the task mattered.

Logistics: “Operated pallet jacks and scanners to stage 50+ packages daily.”

Service: “Processed cash and card transactions accurately to ensure fast checkout.”

ATS: Applicant Tracking Systems scan plain text.
Avoid tables, icons, and text boxes in your resume.

1. Opening

2. Proof

2 specific skills or experiences proving you can do the work.

3. Close

Thank you, contact info, and availability for an interview.

08 / How AI Works: Pattern Recognition

The New Test

  • No hand-written rulebook for every cat; humans still design and train the system.

09 / Predict the next word

How AI Works: Pattern Matching

“The cat in the [ _____ ]”

“Peanut butter and [ _____ ]”

“Twinkle, twinkle, little [ _____ ]”

“I would like a cup of [ _____ ]”

AI does not have a human brain. It calculates likely next words.

Vague prompts = generic, robotic junk.
Specific facts = professional, grounded resume bullets.

10 / Job search in the age of AI

The Two-Hurdle Job Search

1. Computer Filter

Applicant Tracking System (ATS)

Looks for plain text and matching keywords from the job description.

2. In-Person Interview

You must explain every word on your paper. Never let AI add a word or skill you cannot defend out loud.

The 3 Golden Guardrails

1. Interview Test: If you can’t explain it, cut it.

2. Hallucination Check: Verify company facts.

3. Privacy Rule: No SSNs or case details.
