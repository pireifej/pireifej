Anatomy of friction · 4 minutes

Two phrases.
One dead end.

✕“You need to do this.”

Command replaces context. The other person has to guess the urgency, scope, and why.

✕“That’s not my job.”

Boundary replaces collaboration. A handoff becomes a wall before the work is even understood.
