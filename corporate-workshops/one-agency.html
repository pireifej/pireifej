Facilitator notes · 60 minutes

1 · 2 min — Welcome

Set the purpose: practical respect across functions, not forced agreement. Name Paul Ireifej as presenter.

2 · 4 min — Closed circles

Use the quote to invite reflection on access, context, and respect. Keep the discussion on the work pattern rather than identifiable people or office stories.

3 · 4 min — Poll

Upload the local QR image before the session. Invite a single, internal gut reaction to the fictional email example. The upload stays only in this browser memory and is never stored or transmitted.

4 · 4 min — Friction

Ask participants to hear both phrases as signals, not character judgments. Keep the focus on the work pattern, not the person.

5 · 5 min — Alignment pivot

Practice “Help me understand” before proposing a solution. Ask for one example of a constraint the phrase might reveal.

6 · 9 min — Lab

Keep the urgent request fictional. Stop any drift into real cases, names, staffing issues, or office personnel examples.

7 · 4 min — In-earshot radius

Ask how visible coordination can reduce surprises while still respecting appropriate privacy and work boundaries.

8 · 7 min — Email landmine

Toggle the email states and have pairs name the practical difference. Do not use actual office emails.

9 · 5 min — Megaphone

Press M only when no one is typing, then enter a room-approved rewrite. Live text is text-only, remains in memory, and should be cleared before leaving.

10 · 4 min — Confidentiality

Read the Zero-AI instruction as workshop guidance. Confirm the office’s actual policy language with leadership before delivery.

11 · 5 min — Hallway test

Invite participants to test a phrase against rank, audience, and urgency: would it remain professional in the hallway?

12 · 7 min — Commitment

Invite one private 48-hour commitment. Start the 60-second timer only after quiet reflection begins. The QR guide is intentionally unconfigured until a local image is supplied.
