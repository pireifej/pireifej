Scenario framing

Slide 4 is an illustrative composite scenario, supplied to make failure modes concrete. No verified incident has been represented. State this aloud before discussion. The value is the review question: how are control health and business impact jointly observed?

Safety and policy framing

Chaos work is hypothesis-driven and authorized. Every experiment needs owner approval, defined blast radius, predeclared success/abort conditions, observability, rollback, and a record of outcome. Fail-open/closed, local caching, and degraded scoring are risk-approved choices aligned to product, regulatory, and customer-impact policy.

AI workflow detail

The loop is a proposed operating pattern, not a claim of autonomous reliability. Human approval is required before an experiment runs. The 0.5% error-budget figure is an illustrative example tied to an agreed 30-minute experiment window; teams must set their own metrics and windows. Deterministic controls should be implemented independently of model judgment.
