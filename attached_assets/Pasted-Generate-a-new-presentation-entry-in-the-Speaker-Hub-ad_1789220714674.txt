- Third-Party APIs: Downstream threat intel / reputation APIs hitting rate limits (HTTP 429) or token revocation.
- Dilemma: Does the system fail open (blind risk) or fail closed (dropping subscriber traffic)?

- CPU Throttling: Microservices hitting resource caps, spiking tail latency past pre-call setup timers and forcing silent fallbacks.
- Connection Exhaustion: Slow database queries tying up pool connections, blocking ingestion queues.

- Pitch: Moving from manually guessing where systems are fragile to automated AI-driven architectural stress testing.

Slide 9: The Resilience Maturity Ladder
- Step 1: GameDays in Staging (Manual failure injection during synthetic tests).
- Step 2: Dark/Shadow Traffic (Replaying live feeds against broken dependencies).
- Step 3: Off-Peak Production Windows (Controlled off-hours injection with monitored rollback triggers).
- Step 4: Continuous Guardrailed Resilience (Automated, bounded chaos testing in runtime).
