De-escalation linguistics

Affect Labeling vs. The “Calm Down” Trap

Avoid: Accelerants

“Calm down.”
“You’re overreacting.”
“That’s not my fault.”

These responses can invalidate feelings and add heat.

Deploy: Affect Labeling

“It sounds like you feel completely blindsided by this update.”

A label can help name emotion and make room for more deliberate processing; it is not a guarantee.
