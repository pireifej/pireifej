---
name: Client resource portal
description: Agreed long-term approach for securely sharing presentation materials with clients.
---

The preferred long-term solution is an invite-only client resource portal. Clients should have individual, revocable access to assigned browser-based presentations rather than receiving editable source files.

**Why:** The portal should make sharing easy while protecting Should Call Paul, LLC materials through access controls, visible licensing, and client-specific presentation assignments.

**How to apply:** Design future sharing around read-only viewing, per-client permissions, access expiration, revocation, watermarking, and concise internal-use licensing terms. Treat password-protected links as a temporary bridge, not the final architecture.
